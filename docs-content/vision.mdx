## The Mission

Our mission with F.R.I.D.A.Y is **not** to build a Jira clone. It is to pioneer an environment where humans and AI agents plan, execute, and observe complex pipelines in perfect synchronization. We believe the future of development is deeply intertwined with autonomous agents that learn, adapt, and execute.

<Callout type="important">
  The focus is on execution context. Agents are only as good as the context they reside in. By integrating the PM board directly with the Agent OS, FRIDAY eliminates context disjointedness.
</Callout>

## Beyond Traditional PM Tools

Today, project management tools are dumb. You type descriptions, move cards manually, and constantly context-switch to Git, Email, or Slack. FRIDAY attempts to reverse this:

1. **Pull-based, not push-based:** If an email relevant to a project arrives, an intelligent worker parses it and automatically surfaces it on your board or as a comment.
2. **Observability embedded:** Running an agent to review PRs? The PM board lets you inspect the exact Langfuse trace for that agent's run right beside the issue.
3. **Enterprise security:** Built to deploy on-prem or air-gapped, using self-hosted LLM variants (via Ollama or vLLM) so no proprietary data leaks.

## Roadmap to v1.0

Currently in MVP, our next phases focus heavily on **Agent swarms** and **Dynamic MCP extensions**, allowing developers to write their own Python/Node servers and have FRIDAY instantly gain those capabilities across the entire workspace.
